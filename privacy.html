Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Who is responsible?

This website is operated by its site author. For privacy questions or requests, contact eml_0073@ni.eus.

What information is used?

The website uses Google Apps Script and Google Sheets to maintain aggregate game-view statistics. When a game page is opened, the system sends the game's identifier, title and genre, together with the visit count and date/time. The counter is intended to measure game popularity; the website does not ask visitors to create accounts or submit personal details.

Google may process technical information, such as connection data, when delivering its services. The website operator does not use the visit counter to identify individual visitors.

Why is it used?

The statistics are used to count game views, order the popular-games section and display view totals. They are not intended for advertising or profiling.

Service providers and retention

The counter is provided through Google Apps Script and Google Sheets, which are Google services. Visit statistics are kept in the associated spreadsheet while the counter is in use, unless they are deleted earlier. Google's processing is also subject to Google's own terms and privacy information.

Cookies and external content

This website does not intentionally set its own tracking or advertising cookies. Games and other external resources may load content from third-party websites; those providers may apply their own privacy practices.

Your questions and requests

For questions about this notice or requests concerning information handled through this website, email eml_0073@ni.eus.
